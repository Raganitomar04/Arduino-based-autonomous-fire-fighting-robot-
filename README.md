# Arduino-based-autonomous-fire-fighting-robot
🔥 Arduino-Based Autonomous Fire Fighting Robot

📌 Project Overview

The Arduino-Based Autonomous Fire Fighting Robot is an intelligent robotic system designed to detect and extinguish fire automatically with minimal human intervention.

The robot uses IR flame sensors to detect fire and identify its direction. An Arduino Uno processes the sensor signals and controls the robot's movement using DC motors. When fire is detected, a water pump is activated to spray water and attempt to extinguish the flames.

🎯 Objectives

- Develop an autonomous fire detection system.
- Detect fire using IR flame sensors.
- Identify the direction of the fire.
- Control robot movement automatically.
- Activate a water pump for fire extinguishing.
- Demonstrate the application of robotics and automation in fire safety.

⚙️ Working Principle

1. The IR flame sensors continuously monitor the surroundings for fire.
2. Sensor signals are sent to the Arduino Uno.
3. Arduino identifies the direction of the detected fire.
4. The L298N motor driver controls the DC motors to move the robot.
5. The water pump is activated when fire is detected.
6. Water is sprayed towards the fire to attempt to extinguish it.
